### Deep Dives

[### Fact-Checking at Scale: Building a More Accurate and Efficient Synopsis Factuality System (11 minute read)](https://netflixtechblog.com/fact-checking-at-scale-building-a-more-accurate-and-efficient-synopsis-factuality-system-d7d85859d808?utm_source=tldrdata)

Netflix automatically checks synopses of its shows for factual errors. Its new factuality service cuts cost per synopsis by 52.4% and raises recall on non-factual synopses from 0.77 to 0.86. Its explanations now overlap about 82% with human annotators' reasoning, up from about 69%.

[### Parquet's Revised Modular Footer Proposal: Row Groups Stop Being Physical Boundaries (14 minute read)](https://www.mail-archive.com/dev%40parquet.apache.org/msg28218.html?utm_source=tldrdata)

A revised Apache Parquet proposal would replace monolithic Thrift footers with independently readable metadata modules encoded as Parquet pages. Column chunks become contiguous page sequences, row-group metadata becomes optional, and statistics can describe arbitrary row ranges rather than fixed groups. Readers could retrieve metadata only for relevant columns, reducing planning overhead on wide tables. The redesign remains under community review and would require changes across readers and writers.

[### Iceberg v4 Stats-Only Fields: How Tables Store Statistics for Partition Expressions (15 minute read)](https://github.com/apache/iceberg/pull/18405?utm_source=tldrdata)

Apache Iceberg v4 introduces stats-only fields that store metadata for derived expressions, such as partition buckets or case-normalized strings, without materializing additional table columns. Statistics are encoded as typed fields in manifest content\_stats structures, enabling expression-aware file pruning. A new file-level tight\_stats flag identifies exact live-row counts and eligible bounds, improving planning and potentially enabling metadata-only computations without scanning underlying data files.

[### Timestral, t0, Tabby, and Morpheus-2.0 Compared on GIFT-Eval (22 minute read)](https://towardsdatascience.com/timestral-t0-tabby-and-morpheus-20-compared-on-gift-eval/?utm_source=tldrdata)

Mistral, The Forecasting Company, Huawei, and JPMorganChase released time-series foundation models between September 10 and October 16. Sean Moran compared them on GIFT-Eval and reran the three with public weights. The top three are hard to separate, with Timestral ahead on CRPS and Morpheus on MASE. Training appears to explain much of the recent improvement.

### Opinions & Advice

[### Is BI Dead? Why Hex Hit Reset and Rewrote Everything for the AI Agent Era (29 minute video)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qs2RvjjqMbo&utm_source=tldrdata)

Traditional drag-and-drop BI is losing its value as AI agents become better at building charts and apps through code. BI's future lies in providing trusted data, business context, and governance so people can generate and use reliable analysis with any agent.

[### Why the Lakehouse Vendors and the Clouds Bet on Postgres for AI Applications (11 minute read)](https://www.kai-waehner.de/blog/2026/11/10/why-lakehouse-vendors-and-clouds-bet-on-postgres-for-ai-applications/?utm_source=tldrdata)

Databricks bought Neon for around one billion dollars, and Snowflake bought Crunchy Data for around 165 million dollars. Google, Amazon, and Microsoft have each extended their Postgres services. This post explains why five vendors with five strategies chose the same database, what the extensions made possible, and what to ask before committing to a Postgres offering.

[### MCP on Raw Data Is Just Excel Hell Again (4 minute read)](https://dlthub.com/blog/mcp-on-raw-data-is-excel-hell-again?utm_source=tldrdata)

Connecting AI agents directly to operational systems through MCP risks recreating fragmented spreadsheet analytics at machine speed. Without governed business rules, agents accessing Salesforce, Stripe, or HubSpot independently can produce conflicting revenue and customer metrics. A more reliable architecture separates an ontology that translates business intent from a precision layer that enforces tested calculations, keeping the warehouse's role as a consistent source of truth.

### Miscellaneous

[### e-means: Outperforming k-means in One-Tenth the Epochs (9 minute read)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.09501?utm_source=tldrdata)

Meta FAIR researchers introduced e-means, a mini-batch clustering algorithm that updates centroids using exponential moving averages instead of repeatedly processing entire datasets. Across embedding benchmarks, it achieves lower clustering loss in 1.4–2.6 epochs than traditional Lloyd's k-means reaches in 25, with reported GPU speedups of 10–45x against optimized implementations.

[### Who is actually using your Snowflake MCP server? (15 minute read)](https://medium.com/snowflake/who-is-actually-using-your-snowflake-mcp-server-683a6cb543d7?utm_source=tldrdata)

Snowflake's query history cannot tell a Snowsight query from the same SQL sent by Claude through an MCP server. This post presents a fix that gives each MCP server its own OAuth security integration, then joins query history to login history on one column to get usage per server, user, and day. It also covers where the approach stops working.
