[### Programming and fatigue (4 minute read)](https://www.natemeyvis.com/programming-and-fatigue/?utm_source=tldrnewsletter)

Programming is much more tiring with AI. This is a sign that you are diligently working on things that require human brainpower and judgment. AI is taking over the repetitive and obvious parts of the job, leaving developers to deal with higher-judgment tasks. It is no surprise that these would be more tiring.

[### LLMs are terrible at designing AI systems (6 minute read)](https://www.seangoedecke.com/llms-are-terrible-at-designing-ai-systems/?utm_source=tldrnewsletter)

Large language models don't know how to design software that uses LLMs. This could be because most training code is pre-LLMs, or RLVR may just not be a good environment to write code that calls LLMs. Also, current AI agents might just not be good enough at software design for the task. LLMs are good and bad seemingly at random, but if you can predict where they will do well or badly ahead of time, you can get enormous value from them.

### Miscellaneous

[### AI Is Causing Signal Collapse Everywhere All at Once (20 minute read)](https://thelastsoftwareengineer.substack.com/p/ai-is-causing-signal-collapse-everywhere?utm_source=tldrnewsletter)

AI is making it increasingly hard to tell good work from bad work and fakes. Humans rely on shortcuts based on signals to make decisions all the time. AI widely undermines these signals and lowers the incentive to produce good work. The speed and scale of the current signal collapse is unprecedented. We need signals to survive in the modern world, and the rapid collapse of many previously dependable signals will create large shifts in society.

[### The Jevons paradox has a second half (13 minute read)](https://hrih.si/articles/the-jevons-paradox-has-a-second-half/?utm_source=tldrnewsletter)

The amount of software being created is going to go up much faster than the number of people paid to make it. AI will enable far more software of a kind nobody used to make. The developer jobs that grow will be the ones that the machines made faster. The demand for software can be bottomless and the work can still move somewhere else.
