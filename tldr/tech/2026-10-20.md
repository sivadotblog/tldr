### Science & Futuristic Technology

[### AI Proved the Quasi-Riemann Hypothesis. What Happens Now? (6 minute read)](https://www.quantamagazine.org/ai-proved-the-quasi-riemann-hypothesis-what-happens-now-20261019/?utm_source=tldrnewsletter)

The quasi-Riemann hypothesis is a weaker version of the Riemann hypothesis, arguably the biggest open question in math. Resolving the Riemann hypothesis would enable mathematicians to better understand the nature of prime numbers. OpenAI's recently released proof for the quasi-Riemann hypothesis is the most significant AI-for-math achievement so far, by quite a margin. The vast majority of the consequences of resolving it already follow from solving the quasi-Riemann hypothesis.

[### Startup taps underwater volcano as energy source in “proof point” test (11 minute read)](https://arstechnica.com/science/2026/10/startup-taps-underwater-volcano-as-energy-source-in-proof-point-test/?utm_source=tldrnewsletter)

Endurance Energy, a Seattle-based startup, recently demonstrated the technical feasibility of using an active underwater volcano to generate electricity. The startup deployed a 3-ton prototype power plant at the Axial Seamount in the northeast Pacific Ocean in September and generated less than 10 seconds of net-positive electricity. The company's next trial run, planned for the winter of 2026 to 2027, will aim to sustain higher power generation. It is currently developing a 2-megawatt commercial generator for its first planned commercial deployment with the Kingdom of Tonga in 2027.

[### Introducing Clairic, a new System One class of extraction models (16 minute read)](https://blog.cloudflare.com/clairic-extraction-model/?utm_source=tldrnewsletter)

Clairic is an extraction model. Extraction models are a new class of System One-style models that can extract, compute, estimate, and forecast values from unstructured inputs, all at significantly higher accuracy, faster latency, and lower cost than traditional LLMs. It answers with values directly extracted from the input, computed from it, or predicted beyond it. The model can't produce a mistyped answer or hallucinate in the same way an LLM can, but it can still make mistakes.

[### Cutting our inference bill by 68% by being obsessive about optimization (15 minute read)](https://blog.cloudflare.com/obsessive-inference-optimization?utm_source=tldrnewsletter)

Cloudflare lowered its average review cost from $2.43 to $0.77. The biggest single saving came from checking what actually reached the provider on each session's first request, and finding a way to order those messages in a way that was as consistent as possible across commits, merge requests, and repositories. Check whether each session's first request actually reads from the cache and whether reviewers still find real problems at the effort level being paid for.
