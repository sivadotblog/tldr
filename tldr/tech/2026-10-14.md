[### Science Corporation implants its vision-restoring chip in its first Stargardt disease patient (5 minute read)](https://techcrunch.com/2026/10/13/science-corporation-implants-its-vision-restoring-chip-in-its-first-stargardt-disease-patient/?utm_source=tldrnewsletter)

Science Corporation's vision-restoring chip, PRIMA, is commercially available in Europe for patients who have lost their sight to an advanced form of age-related macular degeneration, but the company believes its approach can be applied to multiple diseases. It recently implanted the chip in a patient with Stargardt disease, an inherited eye disease that gradually destroys central vision. The company's work with PRIMA is part of a broader pursuit of a novel brain-computer interface. It envisions a future where BCIs expand into a wide range of medical technology applications rather than just being used to control computers.

[### Redesigning source control at Anthropic (23 minute read)](https://www.anthropic.com/engineering/redesigning-source-control?utm_source=tldrnewsletter)

Anthropic was being bottlenecked by developer infrastructure built for human scale, so it built an in-house version control system called Scion to support its next stage of growth, and fully migrated its monorepo from git in five months. It also built and shipped a code review surface, code search, a rich source control IAM system, novel ways of managing and landing large amounts of reviewed code, and more, all in that same time period. Anthropic averaged about 2,500 code changes merged per day in May. It now merges over 30,000 per day, and the rate of growth isn't expected to slow down anytime soon.
