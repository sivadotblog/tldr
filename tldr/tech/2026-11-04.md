[### Siri AI is now open to all developers (4 minute read)](https://developer.apple.com/news/?id=gdk2umrf&amp;utm_source=tldrnewsletter)

[### How software engineering is changing: a tale of two eras (32 minute read)](https://newsletter.pragmaticengineer.com/p/how-software-engineering-is-changing?utm_source=tldrnewsletter)

AI-native startups can ship even faster than fast-moving pre-AI startups because they risk nothing by moving too fast. The old constraints of software engineering have disappeared, allowing startups to move faster, but also break things. The new constraints are too much context switching, losing shared context, tougher pacing, less focus on design, and AI plan limits. Many existing technologies and processes will crack under the change.

[### Interpolative Software (5 minute read)](https://benjamincongdon.me/blog/2026/11/02/Interpolative-Software/?utm_source=tldrnewsletter)

Interpolative software is code that is solidly within the training distribution of models. It can often be specified with just a few sentences of prompting. Novel software is something that has fundamentally not existed before. Writing novel software with agents is challenging, as they are not trained on the task. Not a lot of software is novel, which is fine, as there is still a ton of value in the interpolative stuff.

### Miscellaneous

[### The Death of the Angel Investor (6 minute read)](https://zachholman.com/posts/angel-investor-death?utm_source=tldrnewsletter)

The economics of angel investing have shifted. Many investors have realized it's potentially more profitable to raise a fund instead of using their own money. Technology startups now move much faster, and running around grabbing small checks from angels is less palatable than grabbing a pre-seed fund to fund a whole initial round. Also, when angels try to fund a round, they are often pushed out by the valuation game.

[### Google Will Turn Off Search for Schools That Reject AI (7 minute read)](https://www.nytimes.com/2026/11/03/us/google-search-schools-restrict-ai.html?unlocked_article_code=1.N1E.ptjc.6fOSKNFJlLLf&amp;smid=url-share&amp;utm_source=tldrnewsletter)

Google will turn off AI tools in its software for K-12 students unless school administrators explicitly choose to make them available. However, schools that choose to restrict AI entirely will also lose access to Google search. Google had earlier turned on AI features as the default option and is claiming that the reversal is in response to feedback from educators and parents. The company's decision to use this strategy shows how much power it has over the US education system.
