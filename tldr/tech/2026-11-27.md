### Big Tech & Startups

[### OpenAI may be in cost control mode (2 minute read)](https://micjm.com/blog/openai-is-in-cost-control-mode/?utm_source=tldrnewsletter)

Astra 6.2 and Sol 6.2 have likely been tuned for cost savings. Output tokens per task and reasoning tokens per task have dropped significantly. The models seem to be reasoning a lot less compared to opponents. Charts showing the change in token usage across models are available in the article.

[### Nvidia chip buyers told to rule out Chinese renters to purchase GPUs (3 minute read)](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/26/neoclouds-nvidia-china-ai.html?utm_source=tldrnewsletter)

A new rule that restricts remote access to AI semiconductors was announced by the Trump administration on November 16. The new rule seeks to close an export controls loophole that allowed Chinese companies to rent access to Nvidia's most advanced chips when hosted in data centers overseas. Chip sellers have communicated the message to neoclouds, telling them they won't be able to purchase Nvidia chips if any of the computing capacity will be rented by Chinese companies. Neocloud stocks have come under renewed pressure lately as investors question the sustainability of the AI boom.

### Science & Futuristic Technology

[### It's so over for Quantum Gravity? (6 minute read)](https://nirmalyakajuri.substack.com/p/its-so-over-for-quantum-gravity?utm_source=tldrnewsletter)

A recent paper released by an OpenAI employee claimed to have quantized gravity. The paper quickly gained viral status on X. The employee, who is also currently a postdoc at Stanford, spent 400 million tokens while discussing and proving his results with ChatGPT Astra. However, for now, it appears that AI has not solved quantum gravity.

[### China's second-largest hog producer turns to barn robots (2 minute read)](https://heavytechasia.com/chinas-second-largest-hog-producer-turns-to-barn-robots/?utm_source=tldrnewsletter)

Wens Foodstuff Group, China's second-largest hog producer, has invested in Lasaite Robot, a maker of rail-riding barn robots. Lasaite's robots log body temperatures, feed intake, and the activity of the pigs in each pen to keep livestock healthy. Wens sells tens of millions of hogs every year. The automation provides early warning signals if disease or other health problems are detected.

### Miscellaneous

[### Is Jevons Paradox holding? (3 minute read)](https://openrouter.ai/blog/insights/is-jevons-paradox-holding/?utm_source=tldrnewsletter)

Jevons Paradox has been the defining law of inference so far in the AI boom. Usage has exploded enough to push total spend higher as token prices have fallen. That dynamic has weakened in recent months. Token consumption shows no signs of slowing, but spend growth has slowed significantly.

[### Bullish on Human Intelligence (5 minute read)](https://jiaxin-wen.github.io/blog/bullish-on-human-intelligence.html?utm_source=tldrnewsletter)

Some people believe that human intelligence is obsolete and that all there is left is to build RSI and prepare for an AI takeover. However, despite their recent successes, frontier AIs are still bottlenecked by human talent in getting better. Many of the properties of human intelligence are the moonshot goals of frontier AI labs. People should not give up so easily because of the hype.
