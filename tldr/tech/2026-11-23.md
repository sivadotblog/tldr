[### Tesla launches FSD v15, targets its biggest weaknesses, from signs to potholes (10 minute read)](https://electrek.co/2026/11/21/tesla-fsd-v15-rollout-25x-safety-reality-view/?utm_source=tldrnewsletter)

[### Anthropic Mathematician Says AI Has Solved the Most Famous Problem in Math (5 minute read)](https://www.wsj.com/tech/ai/anthropic-mathematician-says-claude-has-solved-the-most-famous-problem-in-math-9361920b?st=F4ennL&reflink=desktopwebshare_permalink&utm_source=tldrnewsletter)

An Anthropic researcher claims he worked with AI to discover a proof of the Riemann Hypothesis. The conjecture in number theory predicts how primes are distributed. Mathematicians have been trying to crack it for 167 years, without success. The researcher's proof passed verification with proof-checking software.

[### The Merge (58 minute read)](https://new.life.com/stories-details/neuralink-noland-arbaugh-cover-story?utm_source=tldrnewsletter)

Neuralink has increased the rate of surgeries and extended its clinical trials to Canada and the UK. [### AI x Bio is not \_just\_ a data problem (14 minute read)](https://combine-lab.github.io/blog/2026/11/22/ai-bio-data.html?utm_source=tldrnewsletter)

How far AI gets in biology will depend largely on how much data we have. It will also depend on what is measured and the quality of that data. An abundance of systematically incomplete measurements is unlikely to lead to a comprehensive understanding of many of the complex systems that occur in biology. AI will likely become a powerful tool that will contribute to the study of biology, but there is still no clear path to documenting a comprehensive account of how a whole cell or tissue works.
