[### China's Delivery Robots Are Learning to Tackle the Night Shift (11 minute read)](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/features/2026-10-08/china-s-delivery-robots-are-learning-to-tackle-the-night-shift?accessToken=eyJhbGciOiJIUzI1NiIsInR5cCI6IkpXVCJ9.eyJzb3VyY2UiOiJTdWJzY3JpYmVyR2lmdGVkQXJ0aWNsZSIsImlhdCI6MTc5MTc3NDgzNywiZXhwIjoxNzkyMzc5NjM3LCJhcnRpY2xlSWQiOiJUTU0yS0ZWVFRDWlYwMCIsImJjb25uZWN0SWQiOiJBOEExRDhFQTI5OTc0OTRGQTQ1QUE2REJBMjAwNTM3MSJ9.qKwaxiQA41bCTyhO89iik9g1DsGnuCz_-BYe0uX0X2o&utm_source=tldrnewsletter)

Shenzhen opened access for nighttime deliveries by driverless vehicles in March. Night operations make otherwise idle vehicles and infrastructure productive. It also means quieter roads, reduced pedestrian traffic, and lower congestion. Driverless vehicles eliminate the cost of a human: they don't need night shift wages, they don't get tired, and they don't need to be paid extra for driving in the rain.

[### Problems in 22 scientific fields had solutions hiding in plain sight. An AI has found them (7 minute read)](https://www.science.org/content/article/problems-22-scientific-fields-had-solutions-hiding-plain-sight-ai-has-found-them?utm_source=tldrnewsletter)

Borrowing an idea from one research discipline to solve a problem in another is an age-old scientific trick. However, such clever leaps are rare because researchers don't tend to spend their time researching fields outside of their expertise. AI can easily hop between fields. BootLoops is a harness that can spot and solve problems in seemingly unrelated fields that share the same mathematical structures at massive scale. Researchers recently released 36 preprints produced with BootLoops covering a wide range of topics, including figuring out the age of sunspots, analyzing government health policies, and attempting to decipher a mysterious medieval manuscript.

[### So long, Spokes: GitHub rewrites storage to restore reliability, just in time for agentic hordes (5 minute read)](https://www.theregister.com/devops/2026/10/09/so-long-spokes-github-rewrites-storage-to-restore-reliability-just-in-time-for-agentic-hordes/5302148?utm_source=tldrnewsletter)

GitHub is rebuilding its Git storage architecture from scratch. Early internal testing shows a promising 35x write improvement. The plan involves decoupling writes from reads, offloading maintenance, and letting object storage manage redundancy. The change won't alter developer workflows, review processes, or security controls.
