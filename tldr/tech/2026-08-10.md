### Science & Futuristic Technology

[### China-Free Batteries Made From Salt Are Finally Here (8 minute read)](https://www.wsj.com/business/energy-oil/china-free-batteries-made-from-salt-are-finally-here-cc1cd766?st=ZBtRMs&reflink=desktopwebshare_permalink&utm_source=tldrnewsletter)

Peak Energy, a US-based startup, has raised tens of millions of dollars to build a factory for its sodium-ion battery packs. It already has more than $1.1 billion in announced deals with customers. Sodium batteries have less-intensive cooling needs than lithium-ion packs, and they are less likely to catch fire. Another US-based startup, Inlyte Energy, is working on another type of sodium-based battery that can't catch fire at all.

[### The first self-driving vehicle on Mars has proven to be a smashing success (4 minute read)](https://arstechnica.com/space/2026/08/the-first-self-driving-vehicle-on-mars-has-proven-to-be-a-smashing-success/?utm_source=tldrnewsletter)

NASA's Perseverance Mars rover will set a record for the most distance driven by any vehicle on another world sometime next week. The rover's auto navigation system has enabled about 90% of the distance driven by the vehicle to be autonomous. Not having to wait for navigation commands from drivers on Earth has allowed the rover to maximize the amount of scientific return. The vehicle will likely be able to keep driving itself across Mars for many years to come.

### Programming, Design & Data Science

[### Agentic Code Quality (11 minute read)](https://addyo.substack.com/p/agentic-code-quality?utm_source=tldrnewsletter)

Software quality now depends on the constraints set around the agents that wrote it. Constraints define what a system is allowed to do. Setting and maintaining these constraints allows developers to build loops that reliably deliver high-quality production software. Agents can propose anything - constraints decide whether a proposal is safe enough, correct, scoped, and useful.

[### Google's Westinghouse Bet (9 minute read)](https://asimovaddendum.substack.com/p/googles-westinghouse-bet?utm_source=tldrnewsletter)

Google's recent reshuffling of its AI operations has many thinking that the company has failed in the AI race. However, the company may just be refocusing its resources on its strengths. Google Cloud may be a much larger economic opportunity than pursuing its rivals in the frontier AI race. The company's enormous distribution advantage means it doesn't have to compete in the expensive and unprofitable frontier race.
