### Science & Futuristic Technology

[### AI Is Finding Sperm Where Doctors Couldn't (2 minute read)](https://www.nytimes.com/2026/08/11/science/ai-infertile-men-sperm.html?unlocked_article_code=1.41A.Jn5D.uMN3qB1ku73k&smid=url-share&utm_source=tldrnewsletter)

Scientists from Columbia have created a system that uses AI to locate difficult-to-find sperm. Men with extraordinarily low sperm counts often still have several hidden sperm present. Analyzing samples under a microscope with the human eye often misses these individual sperm cells. The Sperm Tracking and Recovery system may bring hope to the 10% of infertile men affected by azoospermia.

[### India's first private rocket reached orbit — from a company that existed before the law did (11 minute read)](https://liftoffasia.com/posts/vikram-india-private-rocket/?utm_source=tldrnewsletter)

Skyroot's Vikram-1 became the first privately built Indian rocket to reach orbit on July 18. It reached success on the first attempt and delivered a payload to about 450 km. India has no space statute, and its entire private space industry runs on executive policy. Skyroot is targeting roughly one rocket launch a month from 2027.

[### His Start-Up's Goal: AI That Is Trainable and Not Controlled by a Big Company (2 minute read)](https://www.nytimes.com/2026/08/11/technology/igor-babuschkin-xai-river-ai.html?unlocked_article_code=1.41A.60Pc.HSJcWvW3aO1r&smid=url-share&utm_source=tldrnewsletter)

The startup wants to create a new type of home computer that allows people to own and use AI without oversight from anyone else, including River AI. [### Elon Musk's New Texas Factory Will Be the Largest Building on Earth, If All Goes According to Plan (3 minute read)](https://www.architecturaldigest.com/story/elon-musks-new-texas-factory-will-be-the-largest-building-on-earth-if-all-goes-according-to-plan?utm_source=tldrnewsletter)
