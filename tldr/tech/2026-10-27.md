### Big Tech & Startups

[### DoorDash Is in Advanced Talks to Acquire Delivery Rival Instacart (2 minute read)](https://www.wsj.com/business/hospitality/doordash-is-in-advanced-talks-to-acquire-delivery-rival-instacart-2b3d3d18?st=vzXBtZ&reflink=desktopwebshare_permalink&utm_source=tldrnewsletter)

DoorDash is looking to acquire rival Instacart in a deal that would value Instacart at around $17 billion. The deal could be finalized as soon as this week, but there is no guarantee it will come together. Instacart's core business involves online grocery shopping and convenience home delivery from retailers. The acquisition will add to DoorDash's food delivery services from retailers such as Kroger, Safeway, and Aldi.

[### The Biggest Takeaways From Anthropic's IPO Prospectus (8 minute read)](https://www.wsj.com/tech/ai/anthropic-ipo-s1-report-takeaways-6eebd721?st=UXMUpV&reflink=desktopwebshare_permalink&utm_source=tldrnewsletter)

Anthropic unveiled its IPO paperwork yesterday, putting it on track to go public as soon as the week before Thanksgiving. The 400-page prospectus tells a story of how the company could usher in a new era of progress that will transform how people code, conduct research, build financial models, and automate routine tasks. It also warned that AI could lead to the development of new bioweapons, destabilize the geopolitical order, and evolve beyond the ability of humans to understand and control. Anthropic plans to trade on the NASDAQ under the ticker 'ANT', with a valuation expected to land between $1.5 trillion and $2 trillion.

### Science & Futuristic Technology

[### 'It's offensively cheap': How solar power is quietly becoming bigger than nuclear (9 minute read)](https://www.bbc.com/future/article/20261023-how-solar-became-bigger-than-nuclear?utm_source=tldrnewsletter)

Solar power will likely provide 10% of the world's power by the end of the year. Solar electricity is the fastest-growing source of new electricity in history as it has become unbelievably cheap. China is still the world's biggest solar power producer by far, but the installations in the US are skyrocketing. Even with tariffs, solar is still one of the cheapest sources of new power in the US, and it is getting built regardless of political lines.

[### Working with academic communities to build scientific understanding (12 minute read)](https://openai.com/index/building-scientific-understanding-with-academic-communities/?utm_source=tldrnewsletter)

OpenAI has announced three new discoveries in mathematics, computer science, and physics. For the math result, which is about harmonic analysis, the AI lab will release the AI output, so researchers are free to organize their own efforts to explain the result. OpenAI will form an external group of collaborators to explain the computer science result, which is about complexity theory, and will make the AI output publicly available. The lab will also form an external group of collaborators for the physics result, which is about quantum field theory, but defer to them on the release of the materials.

[### How the Claude Code team does code review (18 minute read)](https://claude.dev/blog/solving-code-review-at-anthropic/?utm_source=tldrnewsletter)

Claude writes about 90% of the code in Claude Code. This post explains how the team reviews the code at the volume its agents produce. The team moved most of the reading in the review stage to machines, optimized for human time and attention, and invested heavily in making it fast and easy to undo mistakes. This resulted in merged pull requests to Claude Code's codebase more than doubling while the share that had to be reverted fell by more than 40% between June and September.

[### The Counting House (49 minute read)](https://yegge.ai/essays/the-counting-house/?utm_source=tldrnewsletter)

The Counting House pattern adds AI to systems that handle large volumes of human work, notably customer service departments. It can be used to control and improve costs, quality, and human ramp-up time using comparatively cheap AI models. A Counting House uses classic data science and engineering to put lower-tier models on hardened rails to support human workflows. It allows teams to measure and report how well models do against their human counterparts.

[### The End of the Internet (18 minute read)](https://stratechery.com/2026/the-end-of-the-internet/?utm_source=tldrnewsletter)

Blogging began to die when Google started aggregating the internet. Social media made it much easier to create and share content, but this caused the amount of content to increase by orders of magnitude. Algorithms started to matter, and then these sites started becoming personal entertainment sites, tuned for engagement. The latest series of security vulnerabilities unveiled by AI will further shrink the public internet.

[### Microsoft Will Offer Buyouts to 60,000 Employees (3 minute read)](https://www.wsj.com/tech/microsoft-will-offer-buyouts-to-60-000-employees-4c32945f?mod=tech_trendingnow_article_pos1&utm_source=tldrnewsletter)

Microsoft is extending voluntary buyout offers to 60,000 employees in Canada, India, the UK, and the US. The program is time-limited. It provides eligible employees with financial support and career planning services. Microsoft expects less than 30% of employees will accept the latest buyouts. The voluntary retirement program is part of a broader shift by Microsoft to alter its performance system and how it awards bonuses and stock options.
