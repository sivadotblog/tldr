[### Trump sealed deal for Nvidia to rehire Jensen Huang as CEO (4 minute read)](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/23/trump-nvidia-jensen-huang.html?utm_source=tldrnewsletter)

President Trump made a personal promise that Jensen Huang had not broken national security laws. The sharp stock price drop after his resignation and questions about the leadership of the interim CEO also spurred the board's decision. Nvidia's newly appointed board chair had previously stated that the board did not intend to reinstate Huang as CEO. Trump believes his administration's close working relationship with Huang gives the US its best opportunity to protect national security and prevent cutting-edge AI chips from reaching banned customers.

### Science & Futuristic Technology

[### Tesla FSD v15 is AI4's last shot as Musk reveals how little compute is left (5 minute read)](https://electrek.co/2026/11/23/tesla-fsd-v15-ai4-last-shot-compute-limit/?utm_source=tldrnewsletter)

Tesla's FSD v15 is the company's last real shot at delivering unsupervised self-driving on the AI4 computer. The next-gen A15 chip will not be coming to cars, and the mid-cycle AI4.5 upgrade had its RAM cut from 32GB to 24GB. The mid-cycle refresh exists mainly because Tesla needs a new memory type it can actually buy. The cost of the AI4 chip is now twice as much due to memory and storage price hikes.

[### The Incredible, Elegant DNA Repair System Makes Gene Editing Work (12 minute read)](https://www.quantamagazine.org/the-incredible-elegant-dna-repair-system-makes-gene-editing-work-20261123/?utm_source=tldrnewsletter)

Cells have a native repair mechanism for DNA that recognizes and replaces faulty DNA before lasting damage or dysfunction can set in. This ability evolved essentially at the same time as DNA, as it is impossible to have stable genetic information without DNA repair. The mechanisms are essential to the evolutionary lineage of all known life. Scientists and doctors experimenting with gene editing use these natural self-editing and repair tools to insert, delete, or mutate genes to study and treat disease.

[### Ladybird Web Browser/Engine Reaches Alpha (2 minute read)](https://www.phoronix.com/news/Ladybird-Browser-Alpha?utm_source=tldrnewsletter)

The Ladybird open source desktop web browser was built from scratch with its own independent web engine. After four years of dedicated development, it has now entered its alpha phase. Usability is still modest, but the browser is functional. Some websites will still crash. The alpha build is available for macOS and Linux.
