### Science & Futuristic Technology

[### Experimental mRNA therapy promotes repair after heart attack damage (3 minute read)](https://medicalxpress.com/news/2026-11-experimental-mrna-therapy-heart.html?utm_source=tldrnewsletter)

Researchers have developed a treatment that uses mRNA therapy to reawaken heart muscle cells so that they start to divide and help repair damaged muscle. The treatment helped mice produce stronger heartbeats following a heart attack. While not a full cure for heart disease, even small improvements to heart function have been linked to lower mortality. The treatment is short-lived and localized, and is not expected to cause any permanent modification to the heart cells. Further trials in larger animal models are required before human testing can begin.
