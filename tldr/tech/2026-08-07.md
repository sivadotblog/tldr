### Science & Futuristic Technology

[### This AI Just Created Viruses Not Found in Nature (8 minute read)](https://www.nytimes.com/2026/08/06/science/ai-viruses-bacteria-arc.html?unlocked_article_code=1.3lA.pm4x._XyPxLroIp7d&smid=url-share&utm_source=tldrnewsletter)

Researchers have used AI to create new kinds of viruses for the first time. They taught AI to recognize patterns of DNA structure in nature and then use that data to write recipes for entirely new viruses. The researchers followed those recipes to create DNA molecules, which they inserted into bacteria. The viruses were able to infect other bacteria, demonstrating that they were viable.

[### Nuclear startup Oklo splits its first atoms in test reactor (3 minute read)](https://www.canarymedia.com/articles/nuclear/nuclear-startup-oklo-splits-its-first-atoms-in-test-reactor?utm_source=tldrnewsletter)

Oklo's Groves Isotope Test Reactor has sustained a chain reaction. The reactor is a demonstration version of the technology Oklo plans to use for its new medical isotope business. The company also plans to expand into recycling nuclear waste, fabricating its own fuel, and producing medical isotopes. Oklo is preparing a site near the Idaho National Laboratory for its first small modular reactor but does not yet have permission to work on the reactor itself.

[### Agent Plugins (2 minute read)](https://agent-plugins.org/?utm_source=tldrnewsletter)

Agent Plugins is a portable package format for reusable components that extend AI agents. Its version 1.0.0 specification defines a shared format for Agent Skills and MCP servers that compatible clients can discover and load consistently. Agent Plugins defines a small interoperability floor for the parts that can be portable across clients. Shared components can use one predictable structure, while distribution, installation, permissions, user experience, and client-specific capabilities remain under each client's control.

[### AMD acquires AI chip startup Taalas to boost inference performance by etching models into silicon (6 minute read)](https://www.theregister.com/systems/2026/08/06/amd-acquires-ai-chip-startup-taalas-to-boost-inference-performance-by-etching-models-into-silicon/5284344?utm_source=tldrnewsletter)

AMD has acquired AI chip company Taalas for an undisclosed sum. The deal is expected to close in the fourth quarter, subject to regulatory approval. Taalas bakes model weights directly into silicon to boost inference performance by an order of magnitude or more. The company's first chip, the HC1, can serve Meta's Llama3.1 8B at a blistering 16,860 tokens a second, 48x faster than Nvidia's GPUs when it was announced last February.

GitHub Actions went into a major outage yesterday. Webhook triggers were throttled, so pushes and pull requests quietly don't start anything at all. Runners were being handed jobs that no longer existed. Both hosted and self-hosted were affected. GitHub's record outages show that the platform is growing faster than it can handle.
