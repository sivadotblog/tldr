### Science & Futuristic Technology

[### I've been drinking wine in my kitchen (3 minute read)](https://scottaaronson.blog/?p=10362&amp;utm_source=tldrnewsletter)

AI has been solving long-standing problems in mathematics at an increasing rate. These proofs come with formal certifications that prove their correctness to a level beyond what would normally be demanded for a math paper published in a journal. Many people will try to deny that these proofs are valid, but experts agree there aren't any real technical issues with the proofs. The phenomenon is causing some mathematicians to rethink their lives.

[### The Most Famous Problem in Math Was Just Solved by AI—and This Human (10 minute read)](https://www.wsj.com/tech/ai/riemann-hypothesis-anthropic-math-levent-alpoge-75351bcb?st=o1qGTt&amp;reflink=desktopwebshare_permalink&amp;utm_source=tldrnewsletter)

Levent Alpöge graduated from Harvard with the highest GPA in his class, won the prize for America's top young math researcher, and earned his Ph.D. in math from Princeton before he took a job at Anthropic. He recently stepped into the public limelight due to his claim of having solved the Riemann Hypothesis with the help of AI. His 17-million-line-long proof has already been verified by two independent groups. If it holds up to further scrutiny, Alpöge will become one of the most prominent mathematicians in history.
