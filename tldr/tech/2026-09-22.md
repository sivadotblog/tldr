### Big Tech & Startups

[### SpaceXAI releases Grok 4.7 for coding and knowledge work (2 minute read)](https://www.testingcatalog.com/spacexai-releases-grok-4-7-for-coding-and-knowledge-work/?utm_source=tldrnewsletter)

SpaceXAI has released Grok 4.7, a model for coding and knowledge work. Access is now open in Cursor, Grok Build, the Grok API, third-party coding harnesses, model routers, and cloud platforms. Pricing starts at $2 per million input tokens and $6 per million output tokens. There is a 'fast' variant that offers twice the output speed at twice the price.

[### Googlebooks launch October 4 starting at $899—here are the five models you can preorder today (15 minute read)](https://arstechnica.com/gadgets/2026/09/googlebook-laptops-launch-october-4-starting-at-899-preorders-for-five-models-live-today/?utm_source=tldrnewsletter)

Google's new Android-powered Googlebooks laptops are intended to be 'real' computers with premium hardware, on-device apps, and powerful development tools. The first Googlebooks are now available from several OEMs. While Chromebooks were mostly budget machines, Googlebooks are priced much higher, with only one model under a grand. AI is a core part of the Googlebooks experience - Gemini models are going to be deeply integrated with the software.

### Science & Futuristic Technology

[### A Rocket Supply Crunch Is Making It Harder to Hitch a Ride to Space (7 minute read)](https://www.wsj.com/business/a-rocket-supply-crunch-is-making-it-harder-to-hitch-a-ride-to-space-883f5e53?st=bHK6VH&reflink=desktopwebshare_permalink&utm_source=tldrnewsletter)

Many rockets under development are behind schedule. Prices for many missions are rising. SpaceX is winding down the Falcon 9 to focus on its new Starship vehicle. The company has stopped selling missions where several companies share space on a Falcon 9. Launch demand is set to outstrip supply at least through the end of the decade.

[### Agility Robotics: Inside the First US Humanoid Company to Go Public (7 minute read)](https://www.tanayj.com/p/agility-robotics-inside-the-first?utm_source=tldrnewsletter)

Agility Robotics is going public through a merger with Michael Klein's Churchill Capital XI at a $2.5 billion pre-money valuation. The company's robots are deployed in a few places today and can do tasks such as moving totes, feeding lines, and tending machines. Its Digit v5 robot will be out later this year. The new robot will be able to work alongside humans rather than in a cage/workcell. It will also support fast charging, a higher payload, and swappable end effectors.

[### Jev introduces a new shape of LLM—System One, aka Decision Models (5 minute read)](https://simonwillison.net/2026/Sep/21/jev/?utm_source=tldrnewsletter)

Jev is a new category of model called 'System One models' that accepts text inputs, but only returns floating point numbers corresponding to categories, yes/no questions, ratings, and associated confidence scores. It is fast and really cheap - output is free, and input is $0.042 per million tokens. The model was released just under a week ago, but the amount of activity around it has been extremely impressive.

[### An undercover Google analyst infiltrated a notorious supply-chain hacking gang (13 minute read)](https://arstechnica.com/security/2026/09/an-undercover-google-analyst-infiltrated-a-notorious-supply-chain-hacking-gang/?utm_source=tldrnewsletter)

The hacker group known as TeamPCP tainted hundreds of open-source programs with its malware, stole developer accounts to perpetuate that software supply-chain hacking, and released a Dune-themed worm to automate the process. They ultimately breached more than a thousand companies. During a key moment of the group's rampage, an undercover researcher from Google infiltrated the group. This allowed Google to monitor the hacking spree from the inside, warn targets, and help disrupt TeamPCP's attempts to exploit victims.
