[### Jensen Huang Has Been Ousted as Nvidia CEO (8 minute read)](https://www.wsj.com/business/nvidia-is-preparing-to-oust-nvidia-ceo-jensen-huang-30f421ad?st=rquLjB&amp;reflink=desktopwebshare_permalink&amp;utm_source=tldrnewsletter)

Jensen Huang has resigned as Nvidia's CEO after the revelation of two extramarital affairs. Finance chief Colette Kress will serve as interim CEO until a permanent successor is named. The board is considering both internal and external candidates. Nvidia's board has appointed long-time director Harvey C. Jones as its first ever chair. Huang has previously said that he doesn't believe in succession planning and that leaders should continuously pass on knowledge and information as they have it.

[### The Next Chapter of mRNA (28 minute read)](https://time.com/article/2026/11/12/next-chapter-of-mrna-moderna-stephane-bancel/?utm_source=tldrnewsletter)

mRNA technology has the potential to generate hundreds of new treatments for a range of diseases. The country runs more clinical trials than any place else. Chinese drugmakers with global ambitions currently pursue them primarily through partnerships with Western companies, who usually take charge of late-stage development and eventual sales of the drugs outside of China. Some lawmakers in the US are calling for restrictions on partnerships with Chinese companies, but industry analysts say the business momentum is too strong to break.

[### AI Company Employees Should Unionize (10 minute read)](https://www.astralcodexten.com/p/ai-company-employees-should-unionize?utm_source=tldrnewsletter)

Safety-conscious employees at AI companies should unionize, as this would give them the power to keep their employers in check if they have concerns about their work. There are so few skilled AI researchers that companies will want to negotiate if a dozen top AI researchers threaten to walk. A union would uphold normal protections around discipline and firing, demand transparency, and act as an independent voice to hold companies to their commitments. RSI could make AI employees obsolete as soon as next year, so they need to take action now, as they may never have this amount of leverage again.
