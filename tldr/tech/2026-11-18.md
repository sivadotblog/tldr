Zipline and Uber Eats have launched a commercial drone food delivery service in Spring, Texas. Drone delivery will be limited to participating restaurants at launch. The drones can carry orders that weigh up to eight pounds. They can fly at about 300 feet above the ground at speeds of up to 70 miles per hour.
