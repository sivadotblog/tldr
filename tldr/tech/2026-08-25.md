[### Data centers become “killer application” for new power transformer tech (7 minute read)](https://arstechnica.com/gadgets/2026/08/energy-hungry-ai-data-centers-spur-new-power-transformer-technology/?utm_source=tldrnewsletter)

The AI data center boom has accelerated investment into solid-state power transformers. This technology relies on high-frequency semiconductor switching to perform voltage conversions and can be mass-manufactured using semiconductor materials such as silicon carbide. Solid-state power transformers are physically smaller and lighter than conventional transformers and have modular designs that enable easy upgrades and replacements of parts. They can directly convert AC power from a local grid's distribution network into the DC power that data centers require.

[### The State Machine Nobody Designed (17 minute read)](https://www.vedanshh.com/blog/context-engineering?utm_source=tldrnewsletter)

Every part of an agent harness adds information to the model's context, controls what information is allowed into that context, or checks the model's output after it is generated. This is just context engineering. Thinking in terms of harnesses makes developers think in terms of adding components. Context is limited, so it's not just about what you can add, but about what's worth keeping as well. The context window is the whole state machine.
