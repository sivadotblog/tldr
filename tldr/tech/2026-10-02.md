### Big Tech & Startups

[### Starlink 'Community Site' Program Teases Hourly, Weekly Internet Passes (2 minute read)](https://www.pcmag.com/news/starlink-community-site-program-teases-hourly-weekly-internet-passes?utm_source=tldrnewsletter)

Starlink plans to offer hourly, daily, and weekly passes for its satellite internet service. The company is recruiting 'hosts' to sell passes to interested consumers. Pricing details have yet to be released. Starlink will take care of payments, user access, and connectivity while the hosts focus on setting up their sites and earning from connections.

[### Why Muse May Never Need Ads (13 minute read)](https://www.mbi-deepdives.com/no-ads-muse/?utm_source=tldrnewsletter)

Meta is one of the very few companies that can monetize a personal agent through advertising without ever showing an ad inside the agent itself. The company can keep ads out of the Muse app and still get paid for it on Facebook and Instagram. Every Muse errand can be an incremental signal. Meta can indirectly monetize Muse until enough demand has been aggregated for it to start with direct monetization.

### Science & Futuristic Technology

[### World's first enhanced geothermal power plant completed in just 23 months (2 minute read)](https://techcrunch.com/2026/10/01/worlds-first-enhanced-geothermal-power-plant-completed-in-just-23-months/?utm_source=tldrnewsletter)

Fervo Energy has started selling electricity from its Cape Station geothermal power plant. It is the first enhanced geothermal company to reach a key commercial milestone. The entire site has the potential to generate as much as 4 gigawatts of electricity. The first block at the site took 23 months to complete - Fervo aims to complete future blocks in as little as 18 months.

[### With PRIMA, NASA will try to build a billion-dollar space telescope in record time (6 minute read)](https://arstechnica.com/space/2026/09/nasa-green-lights-billion-dollar-infrared-observatory-for-launch-in-early-2030s/?utm_source=tldrnewsletter)

NASA's Probe Explorers are a new line of observatories intended to do more science for less money. The project aims to seek a better balance between the smaller Explorer missions and multibillion-dollar flagships. NASA announced on Wednesday it was moving forward with the first of the Explorers, PRIMA (Probe far-Infrared Mission for Astrophysics). PRIMA will observe the universe in faint, far-infrared light in a range of wavelengths between 24 and 235 micrometers. NASA intends to launch the telescope in 2033.

### Programming, Design & Data Science

[### Pi Durable (33 minute read)](https://earendil.com/posts/pi-durable/?utm_source=tldrnewsletter)

Pi Durable is an experimental package built specifically for long-running, durable, and malleable agents that can run anywhere. It can run anywhere, can be reached from different surfaces, supports infinitely long conversations, survives catastrophic internal and external failures, and lets multiple humans steer the same agents. It is a framework for building any agentic application, including coding agents.

[### RoR creator sparks new “death of coding by hand” debate (15 minute read)](https://blog.pragmaticengineer.com/the-pulse-ror-creator-sparks-new-death-of-coding-by-hand-debate/?utm_source=tldrnewsletter)

David Heinemeier Hansson, the creator of Ruby on Rails, recently announced that 37signals, a company known for its software craft, was done with writing code by hand. The maturation of AI tools has made it possible for the company to embrace a future where coding by hand is almost entirely absent. AI creates new business models and revenue streams and disrupts traditional software. Companies are now investing in innovation and are willing to pay top-of-market for software engineers who can help them build AI products or become AI-native.
