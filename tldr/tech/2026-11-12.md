### Science & Futuristic Technology

[### OpenAI's ‘shepherds' affirm blockbuster physics result (3 minute read)](https://www.scientificamerican.com/article/openais-shepherds-affirm-blockbuster-physics-result/?utm_source=tldrnewsletter)

Experts appointed by OpenAI have verified OpenAI's proof of the existence of the quantum Yang-Mills theory, a vital foundation for the Standard Model of particle physics. OpenAI created a committee to supervise the proof's release after backlash it received when it released a flood of more than 700 AI-generated proofs on a single day in October. This shepherding approach has riled some mathematicians, who see it as an act of needless gatekeeping. The experts do not claim to fully understand the proof yet, as it is too lengthy and complicated for human minds to immediately grasp.

[### Handing over humanity (22 minute read)](https://www.writingruxandrabio.com/p/handing-over-humanity?utm_source=tldrnewsletter)

Some AI experts believe that once we achieve superintelligence, humans will be permanently disempowered, and we shall all be ruled by an AI god. This might not be such a desirable outcome, as humans have a natural desire for freedom. The arrangement will probably not work, as keeping power responsive to human needs has required checks developed over centuries, sustained by repeated feedback from the real world and correction, and there's no reason to keep that equilibrium stable once humans have surrendered all control. Humans have the ability now to steer the technology in a way that will not lead to a complete replacement of human judgment.

[### Gemini Decisions API: Fast, calibrated multimodal decisions (5 minute read)](https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/technology/developers-tools/gemini-decisions-api/?utm_source=tldrnewsletter)

The Gemini Decisions API can evaluate up to 1,000 yes/no, choice, and rubric-scoring questions in a fast, single pass. It can accept text, images, audio, video, and PDF input. Powered by Google's new Gemini Decision 1 model, the model is available through the Gemini API in Google AI Studio. It costs $0.10 per 2 million tokens - outputs are free.
