### Big Tech & Startups

[### Apple's Claris Testing 'Harbor' App for Local AI on Macs (2 minute read)](https://www.macrumors.com/2026/11/10/apple-claris-harbor-app/?utm_source=tldrnewsletter)

Harbor is an app currently being tested by Apple's Claris subsidiary that turns Apple Silicon Macs into private AI servers. It supports connecting multiple Mac computers over Thunderbolt 5 to pool unified memory and enable larger models with higher concurrency. Harbor is in early access and not publicly available. There was an App Store listing, but it has now been removed, so it isn't clear whether the app will be free. The sign-up page for the app asks for a company name, suggesting a business focus.

[### Amazon's Ring unveils flying drone and robot to patrol homes and businesses (3 minute read)](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/10/amazons-ring-unveils-flying-drone-robot-to-patrol-homes-businesses.html?utm_source=tldrnewsletter)

The Ring Drone Cam is a security camera that can fly from room to room to investigate suspicious activity. For $250, the drone can fly up to eight minutes at a time before autonomously returning to its charging dock. The Ring Rover Cam is a $1,000 device with a display and an extendable periscope camera mounted on a two-wheeled base. The Ring Drone Cam will ship in the US on January 27 (pending approval from the FCC), and the Ring Rover Cam will start shipping on December 16.

### Science & Futuristic Technology

[### ‘Flying cars' could launch in China next summer, maker of two-seat model says (4 minute read)](https://www.theguardian.com/technology/2026/nov/10/flying-cars-china-next-summer-xpeng?utm_source=tldrnewsletter)

XPeng's $300,000 low-altitude flying car will be ready for use in the second half of 2027. About the size of a Fiat 500, the vehicle can fly using six drone-like battery-powered propellers. It can be steered using a joystick or fly autonomously to a programmed destination. The price tag includes a six-wheeled van for transporting the vehicle. The flying car has a flight range of 30 kilometers, but XPeng says this will soon be doubled.

[### Starlink Mini 2 Has a Swappable Battery, But It Costs Way More Than Expected (7 minute read)](https://www.pcmag.com/news/starlink-mini-2-has-a-swappable-battery-but-it-costs-way-more-than-expected?utm_source=tldrnewsletter)

The Starlink Mini 2 is the first Starlink dish to support the company's swappable battery, which lasts up to 4.5 hours. SpaceX is asking early adopters to pay $999 to own it. That fee includes a full year of Starlink Roam Unlimited, which is usually valued at $2,100. Shipments are estimated to begin in mid-December.

[### Gemini 4 Carbon is now available (11 minute read)](https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/models-and-research/gemini-models/gemini-4-carbon-now-available/?utm_source=tldrnewsletter)

Gemini 4 Carbon is now available to developers, enterprises, scientists, and consumers. It is available via the API in Google AI Studio and Gemini for Business for all paying developers and enterprises. [### Modern Web Guidance 1.0: The web platform expertise your coding agent is missing (15 minute read)](https://developer.chrome.com/blog/modern-web-guidance-v1?utm_source=tldrnewsletter)

The Modern Web Guidance is a set of skills that embed web platform expertise, best practices, and browser compatibility data directly into coding agents. The stable release of the guidance includes a new modernization workflow, an expanded guide database, deeper human expert review for every guide, and full evaluation coverage across all of the guides. Firefox and Edge have joined Chrome to support Modern Web Guidance to improve cross-browser compatibility and contribute to the project. The project aims to ensure that anyone coding with agents can take full advantage of the power and capabilities of the modern web platform.
