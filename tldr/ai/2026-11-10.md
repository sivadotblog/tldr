[### What objective is reasoning solving? (12 minute read)](https://alexwang.ai/posts/reasoning-as-pathfinding/?utm_source=tldrai)

Reasoning is pathfinding in token space. There are many paths from the problem to a correct answer, and the model traverses each of them with each sample. A path is not guaranteed to converge to the correct answer - it's only in aggregate, over the distribution of paths, do we get a high probability of success. RL training directly raises the success probability. The gains credited to 'thinking longer' come from the search paid for during training rather than at inference.

[### Two Kinds of Test-Time Scaling (28 minute read)](https://www.forethought.org/research/two-kinds-of-test-time-scaling?utm_source=tldrai)

Test-time scaling trades compute for performance at test time. There are two types of test-time scaling: one allows researchers to trade more compute for more performance per task, but only up to the maximum quantity of per-task compute used during training; and the other lets researchers trade more compute for more performance per task, and lets them do so up to quantities of compute and performance that never occurred during training. Scaling methods that permit the latter change will have a larger impact than test-time scaling considered simply. This essay describes how this may impact the world.
