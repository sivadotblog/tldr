### Deep Dives & Analysis

[### Claude was trained on protein sequences at scale (26 minute read)](https://xinmingtu.cn/blog/2026/claude-opus-5-5-protein-sequences/?utm_source=tldrai)

Tests show that the latest Claude models were trained on protein sequences at scale. The models can predict the next amino acid if given the start of little-studied bacterial proteins. No other model tested can do this, and the models fail to predict the sequence if the protein is shuffled. Fable 5.2 beats every open protein language model tested.

[### Measuring alignment eval tells (16 minute read)](https://metr.org/notes/2026-11-16-measuring-alignment-eval-tells/#summary?utm_source=tldrai)

Investigator agents can increasingly distinguish alignment evals from capabilities evals, identifying them as artificial or "honeypot" environments designed to test bad behavior. Opus 5.5 and Astra agents differentiate these by noticing unrealistic scenarios and specific task-level cues. The study's findings suggest that while current models can identify common alignment evaluations, making them less detectable can refine their efficacy.

[### World 2030 (8 minute read)](https://world-2030.pages.dev/?utm_source=tldrai)

Predicting how AI will affect the economy requires looking at all of the different components of that economy and figuring out how they might affect each other. World 2030 is a study that sets up nine groups (AI labs, big employers, banks and insurers, utilities, startups, universities, workers, governments, and the Fed) and runs the US economy forward in six-month turns to 2030. It aimed to identify both the invariants and the boundaries of what can change as these powerful forces interact with each other. The study predicts that AI will break per-seat software pricing and cut US junior desk hiring.

### Miscellaneous

[### OpenAI's first phase of embedded assessments (11 minute read)](https://openai.com/index/our-first-phase-of-embedded-assessments/?utm_source=tldrai)

Embedded assessments give independent experts deep access to a lab's internal models and systems so they can assess model alignment and the effectiveness of safeguards over an extended period. The access gives assessors the evidence and context to pursue independent evaluations and uncover unanticipated failures. Public reporting from independent assessments can give the public a clearer view of how labs are evaluating and managing risks. OpenAI has signed agreements with four independent assessors for its first phase of embedded assessments.

[### The frontier is still open (16 minute read)](https://research.perplexity.ai/?utm_source=tldrai)

Perplexity Frontier Research is a new initiative at Perplexity that aims to build new algorithms, models, and evaluations in the open, with the mission of building an automated scientist. Applications are now open until December 5. The research effort will be supported by 10,000 GPUs. Results from the initiative will ship as papers, blog posts, model weights, and open repositories.
