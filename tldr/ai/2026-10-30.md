### Deep Dives & Analysis

[### AI can turn security fixes into working exploits before users receive an update (16 minute read)](https://epoch.ai/publications/open-weight-models-patch-gap?utm_source=tldrai)

GLM 5.3 can identify and exploit vulnerabilities in recent versions of the Mozilla Firefox browser using security fixes that have been published but not yet shipped to users. Widely available open-weight models can now do much, or even all, of this work on their own. Open-source projects are particularly vulnerable to this kind of attack as their code is publicly available. This threat vector will likely worsen as open-weight cyber capabilities continue to improve.

[### The surprising leadership dynamics in agent swarms (17 minute read)](https://www.raindrop.ai/blog/agent-swarm-leadership/?utm_source=tldrai)

Agent swarms are groups of agents that work together to accomplish a task. Researchers created an experiment in which eight agents from four different models worked together to resolve simulated outages to understand how agents organize themselves in a swarm without a designated leader. They discovered two factors that could influence an agent's potential leadership: the agent's underlying models, and the order in which their name was listed in a prompt. This suggests that even seemingly minor details in how an agent swarm is configured can affect how agents coordinate.

### Miscellaneous

[### Why Sam Altman is against a single superintelligence (64 minute read)](https://cheekypint.substack.com/p/why-sam-altman-is-against-a-single?utm_source=tldrai)

Sam Altman would rather have a trillion AIs of slightly superhuman intelligence join society than one AI of 1 trillion IQ join society. Swarms of agents match a lot of the properties of a resilient society. They keep each other in check and collaborate to work towards goals. AI research shouldn't lead in a centralized direction because agents pushing against each other creates a dynamic equilibrium.

[### The Economics of Intelligence: How and when does AGI lead to economic transformation? (24 minute read)](https://institute.deepmind.com/essays/the-economics-of-intelligence/?utm_source=tldrai)

AI models are getting exponentially better in individual competence while lagging behind in coordination. Models already match or outperform experienced specialists on self-contained deliverables across dozens of professions. The labor market reflects the current capabilities of AI systems. Wages and job roles will be the earliest signals of change.
