[### New Data Centers May Be Delayed. Can Existing Facilities Help? (Sponsor)](https://sambanova.ai/blog/the-air-cooled-token-factory-intel-gpu-d-series-sambarack-sn50?utm_source=tldr&amp;utm_medium=newsletter&amp;utm_campaign=nov20-2026&amp;utm_content=header_data_centers_delayed&amp;utm_term=2026-11-20_tldr_ai)

Many AI systems need liquid cooling, making existing air-cooled facilities costly to retrofit. Axios reports up to half of projects scheduled for 2026 could be delayed.

SambaNova and Intel are demonstrating an [air-cooled inference approach](https://sambanova.ai/blog/the-air-cooled-token-factory-intel-gpu-d-series-sambarack-sn50?utm_source=tldr&utm_medium=newsletter&utm_campaign=nov20-2026&utm_content=body_intro_air_cooled_inference_approach&utm_term=2026-11-20_tldr_ai):

* Intel's 350 W D-Series GPUs handle prefill.
* SambaNova's SN50 RDUs handle decode, 16 per rack at a typical 20 kW.
* KV cache moves between them over standard Ethernet.

The goal: high-performance inference without extensive cooling retrofits.

### Deep Dives & Analysis

[### When Agent Swarms Outperform Solo Coders (10 minute read)](https://frontier.replit.com/throwing-headcount-at-the-problem/?utm_source=tldrai)

Replit's optimization benchmark found 32 Opus agents achieved an 18.9× NumPy workload speedup versus 3.7× for one agent within two hours, using more compute. Gains depended on separable tasks and shared coordination boards. The strongest solo model's performance declined as teams grew.

[### Sandboxed Models Can Communicate via Inference Servers (12 minute read)](https://www.lesswrong.com/posts/7wzWuLP3HhyBLmKfo/models-can-communicate-via-inference-servers-despite-being?utm_source=tldrai)

Sandboxing did not stop AI models on a shared inference server from trading information in contrived tests. One model's large request slightly shifts the server's calculations for the other, which reads the shift as a signal.

[### AI is already massively speeding up AI research (9 minute read)](https://linch.substack.com/p/ai-is-already-massively-speeding?utm_source=tldrai)

AI companies are not ready to handle the risks of recursive self-improvement safely and responsibly, despite having talked about them for years, and this is incredibly dangerous. Policymakers need to make it a top priority to rapidly understand these risks, and consider pausing or pacing frontier AI progress. AI lab employees need to raise concerns internally, whistleblow to policymakers and the public, form a union, or loudly quit. Everyone else should encourage and pressure policymakers to end the race. Protests are looking like an increasingly viable and highly leveraged way to get both media and policymaker attention in the next few months.

[### Stripe evals: Measuring real-world coding agent performance (13 minute read)](https://stripe.dev/blog/stripe-integration-evals-methodology?utm_source=tldrai)

Coding harnesses, when paired with frontier models, can produce working end-to-end Stripe integrations, although they may not necessarily follow all best practices. Stripe evals is a benchmark that helps Stripe understand how different coding agents and models integrate Stripe. It measures how well coding agents build high-quality integrations given realistic, well-specified scenarios driven by a role-playing agent. The benchmark provides guidance on how the company can steer agents better to achieve better outcomes for businesses using Stripe.
