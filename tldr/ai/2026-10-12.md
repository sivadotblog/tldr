### Deep Dives & Analysis

[### AI Scaling vs Human Scaling (12 minute read)](https://www.tobyord.com/writing/ai-scaling-vs-human-scaling?utm_source=tldrai)

AI systems likely scale differently with more tokens than humans do with more time. This ability to scale has improved as newer models are released. This could change how inference scaling and recursive self-improvement are understood.

[### What is Speculative Decoding? (70 minute read)](https://astralord.github.io/posts/speculative-decoding-the-art-of-guessing-well/?utm_source=tldrai)

Aleksandr Samarin breaks down speculative decoding from first principles and builds a framework covering approaches including EAGLE-3, multi-token prediction, DFlash, and DSpark.

[### Do Agent Teams Pay Off? A Case Study on Vibe Code Bench (18 minute read)](https://www.vals.ai/blogs/multi-agent-vibe-code-bench?utm_source=tldrai)

Researchers ran tests on GPT-6 Sol and Claude Opus 5.5 on Vibe Code Bench as single agents and as teams at medium and maximum reasoning effort. Teams cost 1.8 to 5.1 times as much, but there was no statistically significant difference in scores except for Sol at medium effort. Whether a team was worth it depended on the model, task, and budget. Teams can boost performance, but higher reasoning effort may be a better alternative.

[### Who funds the independent AI evaluators? (12 minute read)](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/10/11/ais-quiet-safety-gatekeepers-are-stepping-into-the-spotlight.html?utm_source=tldrai)

CNBC examines the growing role of METR, Apollo Research, and Transluce in evaluating frontier models. Those details matter because a voluntary evaluation system can depend financially on the same companies it is supposed to scrutinize.

[### Anthropic asks users to stop being mean to Claude (5 minute read)](https://www.theregister.com/ai-and-ml/2026/10/09/anthropic-asks-users-to-stop-being-mean-to-claude/5302218?utm_source=tldrai)

Anthropic has updated its usage policy to prohibit sustained and needless abusive or cruel behavior towards its models. Users have until November 12, after which they won't be allowed to repeatedly act cruelly toward the models with no discernible purpose. The rule doesn't apply to common versions of user frustration, pushback, dark creative themes, or model testing and research. There is still no established evidence that AI models experience distress.
