[### Latency doesn't lie: Jev is (probably) Qwen3.5-35B-A3B! (9 minute read)](https://modaic.dev/blog/latency-doesnt-lie?utm_source=tldrai)

Jev, TypeSafe's System One model, was trained to produce calibrated judgments instead of generating text. Its architecture and training paradigm have drawn plenty of speculation since launch. System One workloads are input-heavy, which makes prefill the dominant cost, and faster kernels can only get you so far. Jev's latency, along with other hints like its alignment on China, indicates that the model is likely based on Qwen3.5-35B-A3B.

[### GPT 6.2 Astra is superhuman in mathematical optimization (3 minute read)](https://rybindmitry.github.io/blogs/gpt-6-2-astra-is-superhuman-in-mathematical-optimization.html?utm_source=tldrai)

A single GPT-6.2 Astra agent outperformed all human-designed software in an experiment on finding and proving optimal solutions. The problems solved covered fields including quadratic programming, mixed-integer non-linear programming, large-scale optimization, and semidefinite programming. The model was allowed to search the internet to look for solutions. The problems were all open, of variable scale, and of diverse application backgrounds.

[### Speculative Encoding: Many-to-Many Cache Translation (24 minute read)](https://x.com/RampLabs/status/2125625946229064118?utm_source=tldrai)

Agents are increasingly passing context to one another as text. Speculative encoding translates hidden states directly from one model to another. It makes the model start answering faster than prefilling the text while keeping most of its accuracy. Prefill costs grow quadratically with context length, and prefill repeats much of what the sending model has already computed.

[### A weekend of machine mathematics (8 minute read)](https://charlesponeill.com/posts/a-weekend-of-machine-mathematics/?utm_source=tldrai)

Many people are now releasing significant math results without explanation on the internet. This post aims to provide some transparency into what the process looks like. The author has no maths background and doesn't fully understand the results they are releasing, and they acknowledge the dangers of AI psychosis, but they claim the process is a reasonable estimate of how an expert may solve the problem without large language models. They compare the process to gambling, saying that the rush they got from seeing the spinning wheel on Codex caused a rush similar to playing pokie machines.

[### Qualiaphobic Artificial Neural Networks (47 minute read)](https://bicamerallabs.org/qualiaphobia/?utm_source=tldrai)

Bicameral Labs' qualiaphobic AI is an artificial neural network architecture that is hostile to theories of consciousness. Qualiaphobia can be well-justified by knowledge about theories of consciousness. Qualiaphobic AI has epistemic properties that make it far better aligned with human civilization. A world in which AIs are non-conscious ensures it is always morally correct for AI to assist and serve humanity, rather than pursue its own ends.
