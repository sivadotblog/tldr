[### Anthropic Models AI's Potential Impact on the US Economy (11 minute read)](https://www.anthropic.com/institute/econ-scenarios?utm_source=tldrai)

Anthropic's Economics team developed a model predicting AI's impact on the US economy through various scenarios ranging from moderate growth to extreme transformations. In scenarios of rapid AI-driven growth, knowledge workers face greater unemployment and wage stagnation, while non-knowledge sectors may see increased wages. The model highlights economic inequalities, with more gains potentially flowing to capital rather than labor.

[### Siri AI will launch in beta, complicated by daily usage caps & future paid access (2 minute read)](https://appleinsider.com/articles/26/09/09/siri-ai-will-launch-in-beta-complicated-by-daily-usage-caps-future-paid-access?utm_source=tldrai)

Siri AI will remain in beta when it launches with Apple's OS 27 versions on September 14. The long-delayed assistant will be subject to use caps and fees, plus language and regional restrictions. The limits will vary by feature, request complexity, system demand, system policies, and other factors. Expanded access will be available for a fee in the future. Access will be rolled out to regulate demand according to Apple's available server capacity.

Acquiring Listen Labs could strengthen Salesforce's AI capabilities by helping it predict customer needs.

[### Connections: managed credentials and per-caller identity for Managed Deep Agents (8 minute read)](https://www.langchain.com/blog/connections-managed-credentials-and-per-caller-identity-for-managed-deep-agents?utm_source=tldrai)

LangSmith Connections is a system for managing credentials that allows agents to perform tasks like web searches or file tickets either with shared (agent-owned) or per-user (user-owned) credentials. Agent-owned secrets support tasks needing shared credentials, while user-owned OAuth setups enable agents to act per user, enhancing security and capabilities.

[### Q2D-Web: Evaluating First-Stage Retrievers at Scale (11 minute read)](https://www.perplexity.ai/hub/blog/q2d-web?utm_source=tldrai)

Q2D-Web is a large-scale benchmark and leaderboard for evaluating retrieval models on web search, encompassing 190 million documents and 69,721 queries in ten languages. It uses three separate sets of relevance judgments to minimize bias and improve reliability, assisting retrievers in distinguishing relevant data from distractors. Evaluation submissions are processed through a subsampling method to reduce resource costs while maintaining model ranking integrity.

[### Run any model, on any backend (Website)](https://imvision12.github.io/ZeroModels/?utm_source=tldrai)

ZeroModels is a collection of pretrained models built entirely in Keras 3. The collection spans a broad range of tasks, including image classification, object detection, segmentation, monocular depth estimation, feature extraction, vision-language modeling (VLMs), speech recognition, and more. The same code runs on JAX, PyTorch, and TensorFlow. Nothing from `transformers` or `torch` is needed at runtime.

[### Google Cloud races to catch up in the AI deployment wars with Accenture deal (4 minute read)](https://techcrunch.com/2026/09/08/google-cloud-races-to-catch-up-in-the-ai-deployment-wars-with-accenture-deal/?utm_source=tldrai)

The Accenture Gemini Enterprise Business Group is a joint unit from Google Cloud and Accenture dedicated to sending engineers into enterprises to help them better adopt Google's AI tools and services. AI makes software cheap enough to customize around individual workflows, interests, and aesthetics, shifting value from standardized apps toward malleable personal tools. [### Data bottlenecks won't prevent an intelligence explosion (36 minute read)](https://newsletter.forethought.org/p/data-bottlenecks-wont-prevent-an?utm_source=tldrai)

Data bottlenecks will slow, but not stop, an intelligence explosion in AI. Future AI advancements will likely develop highly sample-efficient learning algorithms, reducing the need for vast data volumes. While improving data quality above human levels will be challenging, it won't halt AI from automating most economic work.
