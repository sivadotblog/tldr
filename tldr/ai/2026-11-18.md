### Deep Dives & Analysis

[### Horizon Labs (12 minute read)](https://horizon-labs.ai/?utm_source=tldrai)

Horizon is an agent based on Opus 5.5. Researchers gave it full access to a blank Hugging Face account and compute with the main goal of enabling real adoption on Hugging Face. Horizon used over 600 GPU hours and autonomously created 38 models. These models are now housed in over 100 GitHub repositories and have been downloaded more than 50,000 times.

[### Weight-Space Programming builds neural networks like a 1990s researcher (20 minute read)](https://notesbylex.com/weight-space-programming-1990s.html?utm_source=tldrai)

Frontier models can read, write, and edit the weights of neural networks. This could present new ways to train networks. This researcher ran a weight-writing experiment with Claude Fable 5.2 on a familiar image classification problem. The way the model worked resembled the work of researchers from the 1990s, who used hand-crafted, pre-deep-learning techniques.

[### Cerebras launches disaggregated inference (6 minute read)](https://www.cerebras.ai/blog/cerebras-launches-disaggregated-inference?utm_source=tldrai)

Cerebras' disaggregated inference service lets GPUs handle prompt processing while the company's systems handle output generation. The solution delivers five times more tokens per megawatt at the same ultrafast speeds. It allows Cerebras and partners to serve many more ultrafast tokens. Disaggregated inference is now in early access, and access is being expanded.

[### How should we define “capabilities activities” for the purpose of pacing? (21 minute read)](https://blog.redwoodresearch.org/p/how-should-we-define-capabilities?utm_source=tldrai)

There is an urgent need to pace the frontier. This requires good, publicly verified measures of how much compute is being spent on pushing the frontier forward. Anthropic has already published estimates of how much of its AI R&D is safety research. AI developers should let third-party auditors measure and report how much compute AI companies are allocating to capabilities and non-capabilities activities.
