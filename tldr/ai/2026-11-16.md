### Deep Dives & Analysis

[### Quark: our whole training stack, end to end, for $4,200 (7 minute read)](https://empero.org/writing/quark?utm_source=tldrai)

Quark is a language model with about 165 million parameters trained for about $4,200 of compute (about 1,300 H-100-hours). It has the best benchmark average among open models of its size trained on similar token budgets. The model was created to explore every part of the training stack the team was using before spending serious money on a large model. This post looks at the model's data, architecture, pretraining at scale, context extension, reinforcement learning, evaluation, and release.

[### On the Brutality of Amdahl's Law (12 minute read)](https://bosoncutter.substack.com/p/on-the-brutality-of-amdahls-law?utm_source=tldrai)

The world is far too slow to see the high double-digit or more economic growth and seismic transformations in the economy that AI is predicted to bring. Intelligence isn't the only technology curve to go quasi-infinite in the last century and a half. Electricity, communication, computers, transportation, and agriculture have all experienced extreme growth and transformation. Despite this, we've only seen 2% GDP per capita growth for nearly 150 years.

[### End-to-End 4-Bit AI Training (9 minute read)](https://thinkingmachines.ai/blog/end-to-end-4-bit-training/?utm_source=tldrai)

Thinking Machines explored the technical challenges of training language models using 4-bit precision, including quantization errors and their effects on model accuracy. The deep dive examines BF16, MXFP8, and NVFP4 formats.

[### Embla 1 (5 minute read)](https://sevren.ai/blog/embla-1?utm_source=tldrai)

Embla 1 is a reasoning model built for phones. It is designed to be fast and to use few tokens per answer. The model can run on a phone, privately, offline, for free. At 4 bits, Embla 1 averages 57.4 on BFCL, IFBench, AA-Omniscience, GPQA, and MATH-500 and answers in 1.77 seconds end-to-end on an iPhone 18 Pro.

[### Qwen4-Max: Your New Teammate Has Arrived (16 minute read)](https://qwen.ai/blog?id=qwen4-max&amp;utm_source=tldrai)

Qwen4-Max can take on a wide range of creative and professional work in the fields of 3D modeling, game development, precision manufacturing, finance, law, healthcare, and frontier scientific discovery. It demonstrates strong 3D creation capabilities and can use Blender to carry out geometry modeling, scene assembly, material setup, lighting, and rendering. The model works fluently with leading game engines and can handle scene construction, gameplay logic, integration of external assets, material setup, HUD design, and visual effects, and deliver a playable game. Smaller models in the family are coming to open source.

[### Releasing Personal Agent Protocol 1.0 (5 minute read)](https://sierra.ai/blog/releasing-poppy?utm_source=tldrai)

The Personal Agent Protocol enables personal agents to interact with companies through business agents, websites, and APIs. v.0 of the protocol is now available. [### GPT-6 Astra Tackles a Fundamental Physics Problem (15 minute read)](https://openai.com/index/step-toward-defining-the-standard-model/?utm_source=tldrai)

Physicist Nathaniel Craig worked with GPT-6 Astra to develop a candidate lattice formulation of the Standard Model, addressing a longstanding challenge in defining the theory without relying on perturbative approximations. Astra helped construct the mathematical framework and develop a detailed proof supporting the proposed approach.
