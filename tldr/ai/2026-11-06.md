### Headlines & Launches

[### GPT-6 Cyber (13 minute read)](https://openai.com/index/introducing-gpt-6-cyber/?utm_source=tldrai)

OpenAI introduced GPT-6 Cyber, a cybersecurity model designed to identify and validate exploitable vulnerabilities while staying within researcher-defined boundaries.

[### Introducing Inkling 2 Small (37 minute read)](https://thinkingmachines.ai/news/inkling-2-small/?utm_source=tldrai)

Inkling 2 Small is a Mixture-of-Experts model with 594B total parameters, 17B active, that supports image inputs and variable effort. The model's vision and agentic capabilities enable it to incorporate visual feedback to iteratively build and refine websites, games, and 3D designs with interactive elements. It will be initially available through Tinker with closed weights. Full weights will be available under an Apache 2.0 license next week after the completion of external safety testing and red-teaming.

[### Gemini Omni 1.2 Flash (1 minute read)](https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/technology/developers-tools/introducing-gemini-omni-1-2-flash/?utm_source=tldrai)

Google introduces Gemini Omni 1.2 Flash, enhancing speed, cost-efficiency, and extended clip lengths for AI models. This update aims to improve developer tools and effectiveness in AI applications.
