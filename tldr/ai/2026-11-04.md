### Deep Dives & Analysis

[### Training a Text-to-Image Model for $21K (13 minute read)](https://www.linum.ai/field-notes/pjit-21k?utm_source=tldrai)

Linium is a two-person research lab that is trying to train its own generative video models entirely from scratch, aiming to create tools to democratize access to animation. Pyramid-JiT (P-JiT) is a pixel-space architecture that achieves aggressive token reduction through a noise schedule that helps pixel-space models better learn fine-grained detail. This study looks at how models trained on the architecture perform when trained for longer as a pressure test along the way towards larger models and video. Examples of images generated by the resulting model are available.

[### How Much Does Reasoning Effort Affect Score and Cost? (8 minute read)](https://www.vals.ai/blogs/reasoning-effort?utm_source=tldrai)

Lower reasoning effort reduces cost much more than it lowers the quality of results. Tests show that models on low reasoning can match models on high reasoning at lower cost. The impact of reasoning effort depends on the task.

[### Learnings from Auditing Public Evaluations (8 minute read)](https://www.primeintellect.ai/blog/auditing-public-evaluations?utm_source=tldrai)

The article outlines issues faced during public evaluations of LLMs and details improvements made to benchmarks such as AUTOMATIONBENCH and OFFICEQA PRO V2. They replaced ambiguous prose descriptions with JSON schemas for better performance evaluations and introduced new grading systems to improve accuracy. A multi-layered monitoring system is suggested to prevent manipulation and ensure accurate measurement of model capabilities.

[### Progress on provable inference for large language models (30 minute read)](https://www.anthropic.com/research/provable-inference?utm_source=tldrai)

It is almost impossible to verify which model produced a specific output. Cryptographic proofs of inference allow model providers to prove that preach suppose was produced which a specific model. These proofs could enable new applications and contribute to a toolkit for verifying compliance with AI pacing requirements. This post details the progress made on a powerful type of cryptographic proof uniquely fit for provable inference called zKSNARK.

[### Accelerating engineering for the physical world (6 minute read)](https://openai.com/index/accelerating-engineering-for-the-physical-world/?utm_source=tldrai)

OpenAI is leveraging its AI models to enhance mechanical and electrical engineering processes, from design to simulation and production, speeding up internal workflows and improving industry practices. Engineers utilize models like GPT‑6 Astra for tasks such as device design, CAD model creation, and performance simulations, while collaborating with companies like Mazda to explore applications in vehicle aerodynamics.

[### Gemini Notebook API (1 minute read)](https://developers.google.com/notebook/docs?utm_source=tldrai)

The Gemini Notebook API is a REST interface for programmatically creating and managing notebooks and sources in Gemini Notebook. It can turn web pages, YouTube videos, plain text, and Markdown into persistent notebooks. The API and MCP server are in an early access Public Preview. Only personal Google Accounts are supported during the preview - Google Workspace accounts are not yet supported.
