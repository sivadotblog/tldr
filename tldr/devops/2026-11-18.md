### News & Trends

[### Scheduling the removal of the kube-proxy IPVS backend (7 minute read)](https://kubernetes.io/blog/2026/11/16/ipvs-removal/?utm_source=tldrdevops)

Kubernetes plans to remove the kube-proxy IPVS backend in 2026, ending support for that load balancing mode. The removal simplifies the codebase and reduces maintenance overhead for the SIG Network team.

[### Kitesurf is now open source (4 minute read)](https://blog.cloudflare.com/kitesurf-open-source/?utm_source=tldrdevops)

Cloudflare released Kitesurf, its experimental browser engine for AI agents, under Apache 2.0. It parses HTML, applies CSS, executes JavaScript, and renders screenshots on Workers without a browser container, with Chrome DevTools Protocol support for clients such as Puppeteer.

### Opinions & Tutorials

[### Is your OpenTelemetry pipeline becoming a performance bottleneck? (10 minute read)](https://www.cncf.io/blog/2026/11/17/is-your-opentelemetry-pipeline-becoming-a-performance-bottleneck/?utm_source=tldrdevops)

Telemetry collection consumes resources too: excessive attributes, unbounded queues, and expensive processing can turn observability into a bottleneck. This guide explains memory-limiter placement, sampling trade-offs, and why tail sampling needs trace-aware routing when Collectors scale horizontally.

[### Technical Debt is Social; it is the Price of Learning by Doing (3 minute read)](https://www.infoq.com/news/2026/11/tech-debt-learning-doing/?utm_source=tldrdevops)

Technical debt is largely social, a price of learning by doing. AI can generate software faster than teams can build shared understanding of it, making repayment a sociotechnical task.

[### How Salesforce efficiently right-sizes Kubernetes workloads at scale on Amazon EKS (12 minute read)](https://aws.amazon.com/blogs/containers/how-salesforce-efficiently-right-sizes-kubernetes-workloads-at-scale-on-amazon-eks/?utm_source=tldrdevops)

Salesforce combined vertical and horizontal autoscaling across its EKS fleet, reporting more than $5 million in cumulative infrastructure savings. For Istio, VPA adjusts CPU and memory while HPA scales replicas by proxy connection count, keeping the two controllers from reacting to the same signal.

[### Uber's Secure AI Framework Governs Autonomous AI Agents across Their Lifecycle (3 minute read)](https://www.infoq.com/news/2026/11/uber-secure-ai-framework/?utm_source=tldrdevops)

Uber built a lifecycle framework that judges whether an AI agent's proposed action fits the user's request, purpose, permissions, and context. It combines pre-deployment registration, runtime behavioral authorization, and post-deployment replay. Per-agent intents worked better than generic thresholds.
