### News & Trends

[### Cloudflare and OpenAI: making AI discovery better for everyone (5 minute read)](https://blog.cloudflare.com/cloudflare-openai-discovery-agentic-web/?utm_source=tldrdevops)

A research pilot between Cloudflare and OpenAI has enabled the discovery and indexing of hundreds of millions of new web pages. The program used signals from roughly 1% of Cloudflare's network to help ChatGPT find content that was previously unknown. These signals identify useful pages and filter out nonexistent or duplicated content, which reduces unnecessary crawling load for site owners. Cloudflare states the metadata is used for search discovery rather than training AI models.

[### Claude Platform Tracing in New Relic (4 minute read)](https://newrelic.com/blog/news/anthropic-otel-ai-observability?utm_source=tldrdevops)

The Claude Platform now exports OpenTelemetry traces natively from Claude Managed Agents, the Messages API, and the Claude SDKs in public beta. New Relic AI Monitoring reads these traces to link model performance with application data in a single view. Users can add New Relic as a destination in the Claude Console to start receiving spans within about a minute without code changes.

[### Introducing Scout 1.0: A new SRE model powering Bits AI with frontier performance at a fraction of the cost (4 minute read)](https://www.datadoghq.com/blog/ai/introducing-scout-sre-specialized-model-bits-ai/?utm_source=tldrdevops)

Datadog's Scout 1.0 is an AI model specialized for investigating production issues that powers its Bits AI assistant. The model matches the performance of general-purpose frontier models like GPT-6.1 Sol and Claude Opus 5.5 at three to eight times lower estimated inference cost. Datadog trained Scout using reinforcement learning on simulations built from historical incidents.

### Opinions & Tutorials

[### How Datadog Defines Service Criticality at Scale (8 minute read)](https://www.datadoghq.com/blog/how-datadog-defines-service-criticality-at-scale/?utm_source=tldrdevops)

An LLM analyzed several hundred SEV-1 and SEV-2 postmortems for about USD 32 to derive four criticality signals. Services get high, low, or unknown ratings with confidence, owner overrides, and the tiers inform deployment and auto-merge decisions.

[### Finding (and Fixing) a Silent Throughput Ceiling in PostgreSQL (6 minute read)](https://www.enterprisedb.com/blog/finding-and-fixing-silent-throughput-ceiling-postgresql?utm_source=tldrdevops)

PostgreSQL throughput can stall under heavy concurrent inserts even when CPU and storage have spare capacity, as writers queue behind relation extension locks. In a HammerDB benchmark, distributing writes across hash partitions reduced contention and increased throughput 7.2× on the same hardware.

[### Agent-first IDP: how big tech and the clouds are preparing platforms for agents (22 minute read)](https://platformengineering.org/blog/agent-first-idp-how-big-tech-and-the-clouds-are-preparing-platforms-for-agents?utm_source=tldrdevops)

This guide outlines how to evolve an internal developer platform into an Agentic Engineering Platform (AEP) suitable for AI agents. Real-world examples from Block, Uber, Spotify, and public clouds illustrate architectural decisions like managed entry points, agent identity, and trustworthy state. The playbook describes nine practices across four maturity levels, ranging from demand discovery and machine-readable contracts to policy enforcement and domain-specific agents.
